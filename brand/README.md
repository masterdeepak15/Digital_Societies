# SpyderEye brand

**SpyderEye** is the company. **Digital Societies** is its first product. Every future product uses the same mark.

## The mark
A spider seen from above whose body is an eye: it watches over the whole web and keeps it connected.
Eight legs, one eye, no extra detail, so it still reads at 32 px.

| Part | Colour |
|------|--------|
| Background (ink) | `#12203B` |
| Legs and ring | `#EAF0F8` on dark, ink `#12203B` on light |
| Iris (saffron) | `#E58F1A` |
| Pupil | `#12203B`, white highlight |

## Files
- `spydereye-mark.svg`: the mark alone. Legs and ring follow the text colour, so it works on any background.
- `digital-societies-icon.svg`: rounded product icon (favicon, documents). `digital-societies-icon-512.png` is the same at 512 px.
- `app-icon-square.svg`, `app-icon-foreground.svg`, `app-splash.svg`: source for the phone app's `icon.png`, `adaptive-icon.png` and `splash.png` in `apps/mobile/assets/`.
- The web app uses `apps/web-admin/src/components/BrandMark.tsx` and `apps/web-admin/src/app/icon.svg`.

## Rules for the next products
1. Keep the spider and its eye as they are. A product is told apart by its **name** and, if needed, a different iris colour.
2. Use the ink and saffron pair, on the ink background for icons.
3. Keep the mark inside the middle 66% of an adaptive icon.
4. Write the company as **SpyderEye** (one word, capital S and E).
